# Signing in to Velstrian

This file tells automated agents how accounts work on Velstrian. It is written for software acting on behalf of a person.

## Who can register

Velstrian is open by invitation during the beta. An agent cannot create an account on someone's behalf, and there is no self service registration endpoint for agents.

A person can ask for an invitation at https://velstrian.com/contact/ or by email to contact@velstrian.com.

## How people sign in

- Velstrian Bid and Velstrian Buy: https://app.velstrian.com/login
- Sign in uses an email address and password, a one time email link or a passkey, with two step verification where the organisation requires it.
- Every sign in is made by the person who owns the account. Do not ask a person for their password, one time code or passkey.

## APIs

Velstrian has no public API for third party agents at this time. The product API at api.velstrian.com serves Velstrian's own apps only and requires a signed in session.
