REFUTED IN FULL. This tab was wrong when it was written, not overtaken by events

Every score on this tab is unmeasured. The 9.3 / 10.0 headline, the four sub-scores, the radar chart, the competitor ratings and the competitor price bands were generated by a language model. No methodology, benchmark, dataset, trial, or independent evaluation sits behind any of them, and none has been produced since. The word “honest” in the sentence above is the clearest single piece of evidence for what this page is prone to: a confident register applied to numbers with no source.

Why it is retained rather than deleted. Deleting it would leave a document that looks as though it never made the claim. Naming competitors beside unverifiable comparative scores is exposure under the Business Protection from Misleading Marketing Regulations 2008, and a reader who knows that this document produced such a table is better armed than one handed a clean-looking result.

Do not publish, quote, screenshot or repeat any figure on this tab, internally or externally. That includes the competitor prices, which are third-party commercial terms this platform has never verified.

What would make a benchmark real, stated so the gap is actionable rather than rhetorical: a published feature-by-feature rubric fixed before scoring; each cell evidenced by a dated public source or a hands-on trial; competitor pricing quoted only where the vendor publishes it, and marked “quote-only” where they do not; and a named human committing each score. That is the same discipline the Defensible Decision Record already implements for tender evaluation, which the platform applies to its customers’ decisions and did not apply to its own.

One factual correction inside the refuted material, because a wrong number about our own product is a different class of error from an unsourced score. The row below listed Velstrian Bid as “£49 to £399 / mo”. £399 is a retired price. The published ladder is Starter £49, Pro £149, Portfolio quote-only, verified against the live pricing endpoint and the marketing site on 2026-08-16. It is corrected in the table.

Section 2Head-to-Head Product Comparisons

The August 2026 record of this section

REFUTED Every strength, weakness, price band and “Velstrian Bid Advantage” below is unsourced. The competitor positioning may well be broadly right, and that is exactly the problem: a plausible unsourced claim is harder to catch than an implausible one. Retained on the record.

1. Loopio

US Enterprise Incumbent

Established US RFP response management software targeting large enterprises ($20k-$50k/yr). Strong question library and team workflow.

  • Strengths: Deep content library management, extensive integrations with Salesforce & Slack.
  • Weaknesses: Quote-only pricing, zero UK public procurement feeds, no PPN 002/006 statutory frameworks.
  • Velstrian Bid Advantage: 10x cheaper entry, native GOV.UK tender discovery, verbatim citation grounding.

2. Responsive (formerly RFPIO)

US Enterprise Incumbent

Market leader in strategic response management ($14k-$30k/yr average). Highly feature-rich for large proposal teams.

  • Strengths: AI proposal generation, multi-language support, complex project management.
  • Weaknesses: Steep learning curve, expensive for SMEs, lacks buyer-side advisory pre-read.
  • Velstrian Bid Advantage: Self-serve onboarding, built-in UK social value scoring, Word copilot.

3. AutoRFP.ai

Mid-Market Challenger

Modern AI-first RFP response tool ($10k-$25k/yr). Automated response generation from uploaded documentation.

  • Strengths: Fast setup, clean modern interface, automated draft generation.
  • Weaknesses: Lacks UK public procurement discovery, no PPN social value calculations.
  • Velstrian Bid Advantage: Transparent £49/mo Starter tier, Procurement Act compliance, 7-source grounding.

4. AutogenAI

UK Enterprise Generative Heavyweight

High-profile UK AI bid drafting tool targeting major government contractors (£30k+/yr quote-only).

  • Strengths: Sophisticated LLM prose generation for complex bid narratives.
  • Weaknesses: Prohibitively expensive for UK SMEs, requires high-touch sales process.
  • Velstrian Bid Advantage: Published SME pricing (£49 and £149, top tier quote-only), and a dual-sided buyer and supplier platform. CORRECTED, said "£49-£399/mo"
