How email works today (measured by the driver 2026-10-30, do not redesign it)

  • Every email is built in our own code from the central brand tokens and sent through the Brevo API: 32 templates in web/lib/email/templates/*.tsx and the API shell in api/app/services/email_shell.py. Sender contact@velstrian.com (Brevo sender id 5).
  • Sign-in emails (sign up, reset, invite, magic link, email change) go through the Supabase Send Email hook to /api/auth/send-email-hook, which renders web/lib/email/templates/auth-email.tsx and sends through Brevo. Supabase's own dashboard templates are only a FALLBACK if the hook is off.
  • Templates stored in Brevo are retired on purpose (R285-P2-EMAIL-VENDOR-BRANDING-SETTINGS-01, DASHBOARD_TEMPLATES_RETIRED = true in apps/portal/src/lib/api/brevo-client.ts), because a template that lives in Brevo hardcodes colours no repo guard can see (RULE 0-C). **Never create new email templates in the Brevo dashboard.** A missing email is fixed in code.
  • Brevo log, 29 to 30 October: every email since the 29 October promote came from contact@velstrian.com (password reset, reset code, new device sign-in). Older failure alerts came from support@ and support@crowagent.ai, before that promote.
  • Brevo plan: Free, 300 emails a day (owner rule: free tier only). Domain velstrian.com is authenticated. The driver deactivated retired Brevo templates 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7. Templates 1 and 4 are still active because the tool's permission check blocked those two.
  • Staging Supabase Auth has captcha ON, so a sign-in email can only be triggered from a real browser.
