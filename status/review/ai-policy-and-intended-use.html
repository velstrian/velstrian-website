1. Our AI policy

Velstrian helps small and mid size organisations write bids for public and private work (Velstrian Bid), and helps buyers run a fair purchase (Velstrian Buy). AI does part of that work. These are the rules we follow.

  1. People decide. AI drafts, reads and suggests. A person reviews and approves every bid answer before it is sent, and a buyer makes every award decision. AI never submits a bid or picks a supplier.
  2. Your words stay yours. We never train AI models on customer documents, drafts or answers. We learn which answers work by counting events, never by reading your text (D344, api/app/services/ai_quality_events.py).
  3. Facts come from you. AI writes from your own documents and library. When it cannot back a figure, an accreditation or a claim, it says so instead of inventing one.
  4. Only tested models reach you. A model serves customers only after it passes our quality tests for that kind of work, and only from a provider named in our data processing agreement (D328, docs/legal/data-processing-agreement.md Schedule 1).
  5. Plain words. AI text written to you is checked for a plain reading level. Bid text written for a buyer follows the buyer's style.
  6. Fair to every supplier. In Velstrian Buy, AI gives reasons per criterion and the buyer records the decision. Every supplier is read the same way.
  7. We watch and fix. We track quality every week, keep a risk register (docs/release-2.8.5/ai-quality/AI-RISK-REGISTER.md), and can switch any AI feature off quickly if it goes wrong (docs/release-2.8.5/ai-quality/INCIDENT-RUNBOOK.md).
  8. Personal data is kept to a minimum. AI gets only the personal details a task needs, under UK GDPR.

Who is responsible. The owner is accountable for this policy. The policy is reviewed at every release boundary and after any AI incident.

2. Intended use statement

What Velstrian AI is for

  • Finding contracts that suit you, and summarising a tender in plain words.
  • Reading tender documents and listing the questions and requirements.
  • Drafting bid answers from your own documents, for you to check and edit.
  • Checking a draft for figures and claims your documents do not support.
  • Helping a buyer write requirements, read supplier replies and draft letters, with the buyer deciding.

What it is not for

  • Making a bid or award decision without a person.
  • Legal, financial or tax advice.
  • Writing claims about you that your own documents do not support.
  • Any use outside bids and buying, such as profiling people or deciding about employment.

Who it is for. Small and mid size organisations writing their own bids, and buyers running a purchase. No bid training is needed.

What you should do. Read every AI draft before you use it. Check figures and dates against your records. Tell us when an answer is wrong, using the thumbs down button.
