What the company does

Velstrian Tenderworks builds software that helps small and mid size organisations write bids and tenders themselves, for public and private work, without a bid team or procurement training. The bid product reads a tender, finds the questions and scoring rules, and drafts answers from the firm's own documents and past work. The buyer product helps busy buyers run a fair, well documented tender. Plain words, one clear next step on every screen.

Stage: pre revenue. An invite only beta is running. There is no paying customer today (docs/release-2.8.5/ai-quality/OPERATING-MODEL.md section 6, measured 2026-10-29). Revenue, customer and user numbers: OWNER TO CONFIRM, and write none if there are none.

Why AI is core to the product

AI is the main way the product works. It reads long tender packs and pulls out requirements with their weights. It drafts answers that stay backed by the firm's own evidence. It checks each draft against the buyer's scoring and flags weak spots. Every AI call goes through one internal client (AIClient) with a per call ledger, a cost ceiling per feature and quality checks before output reaches the user. The platform already runs Gemini models for most surfaces and Gemini embedding models for retrieval, so Google models are central today, not a plan.

Intended use of Google Cloud and Vertex AI

Taken from docs/release-2.8.5/ai-quality/VERTEX-CREDITS-PLAN-2026-10-26.md sections 2 to 4. In order of priority:

  1. Gemini and Gemini embeddings on Vertex AI in the EU multi-region or London (europe-west2), behind a flag, staging first. Reason: a stated processing location for customer documents, which small firms and their buyers ask about.
  2. Context caching and batch for the large tender reading and drafting surfaces, measured on our own test set before it becomes default.
  3. Document AI Layout Parser as a measured test on the tenders our extraction still misses, capped spend, only if tables prove to be the cause.
  4. Search grounding as a pilot on one background surface (regulatory change watching), showing the source for every claim.

Not planned: Claude on Vertex, Vertex RAG Engine, Vertex AI Search, provisioned throughput.
