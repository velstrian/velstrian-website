Status of this pack, stated plainly

  • Velstrian holds no ISO/IEC 42001 certificate. This pack is our own readiness work. Wording we may use: "We follow the structure of ISO/IEC 42001 for our AI management and track it in our own documents. No certificate is held." (D255 positive wording, D250 no claimed certificate).
  • The standard text is a paid document and was not read. ISO's own page returned HTTP 403 to this lane (https://www.iso.org/standard/42001, 2026-10-25). Price: not seen. Reading a licensed copy is an owner spend decision (spec 10 section 3.1 criterion 4). Until then this map uses only the public structure.
  • Public structure used. Management system clauses 4 to 10 follow the ISO harmonised structure (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement). Annex A is a reference set under objective areas A.2 to A.10, whose topic names below come from secondary guides (https://docs.modulos.ai/frameworks/iso-42001/annexes-a-d.html and https://www.tcsa.in/frameworks/iso-42001/controls, search summaries read 2026-10-25). Those guides disagree on per objective counts and on whether Annex A is normative, so no control count is claimed here and the exact control titles must be taken from the standard before a statement of applicability is signed.
  • Status words: in place, partly, missing. They are the driver's first judgement and have not been challenged.
