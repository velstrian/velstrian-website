The statement (for the security page and the DPA)

Where your data lives. Your account data, documents and drafts are stored in Ireland (Supabase, AWS eu-west-1). Our web application runs in London and our API runs in the Netherlands.

Where AI processing happens. When you use an AI feature, the text needed for that task is sent to our AI providers, Google and Anthropic, under their paid commercial terms. Both commit not to use that text to train their models. Anthropic deletes it within 30 days. Their AI processing is not pinned to the UK or the EU, so we treat it as an international transfer and protect it with the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum. We send only what each task needs.

Notes for whoever uses this text:

  • Transfer risk assessment (TRA). The IDTA or EU SCCs statement for Anthropic (global or us inference) and Google Gemini (not region pinned) needs a transfer risk assessment on file. None is recorded in the repo yet (R285-LEGAL-KIRO-A04-01). Reference it beside the statement once written.
  • "Both commit not to use that text to train" rests on Google's paid terms (pricing page above) and Anthropic's default (training page, 2026-08-18). The owner verified on 2026-10-23 (D314) that both production accounts are paid.
  • Do not write "EU only", "UK only" or "data never leaves the UK" anywhere. They are untrue for AI processing.
  • When the model registry or the OpenAI onboarding changes the provider list, this statement and DPA Schedule 1 change in the same commit, and the web data use line (web/src/shared/i18n/messages/common/data-use.ts:24) with them (spec 12 criterion 15).
