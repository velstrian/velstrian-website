AI operating model

Date: 2026-10-24. Source: specs/platform-systems/ai-quality/17-AI-OPS-SPEC.md criteria 1 to 13, 10-GOVERNANCE-BACKLOG.md section 4. Decisions: D354 (ownership and weekly review), D355 (targets), D356 (dashboard), D357 (change notes), D359 (no refunds for wrong answers), D363 (reading level), D409 (governance readiness now). The spec stays canonical.

1. Who owns what

  • The owner is accountable for AI quality.
  • The driver (the Velstrian platform operator) runs the weekly review and the release boundary list.
  • Response is in UK business hours. There is no out of hours on call. Out of hours, surfaces fail closed through their ceilings and the existing honest failure states, and Sentry alerts on AI route error spikes go to the platform mailbox.

2. The weekly review

Started by hand, never on a timer (RULE 0-G). It produces one dated note under docs/release-2.8.5/ai-quality/weekly/ with: ledger cost and margin share, per surface and per model counters, the dashboard figures against targets, open eval failures, user ratings, support cases, the AI risk register reviewed (spec 10 section 3.4), and the actions taken.

3. Targets

Stored once in docs/release-2.8.5/ai-quality/TARGETS.md, all PROVISIONAL until measured baselines exist: invented figures reaching a bid 0, unheld credentials without a person's choice 0, unsupported claim rate under 5 percent after evidence is added, extraction at the D388 bar, Recall@5 90 percent, faithfulness 95 percent, Buy reasons per criterion and a recorded human decision 100 percent, chatbot wrong price or product fact 0, thumbs up share 80 percent. Win rate is reported to each customer only. No target is published as an accuracy claim.

4. Change process and cadence

  • A prompt or model change ships only with a new prompt version, a family eval from a committed sha, a ledger record, a change note (spec 16) and a tracker row.
  • Prompt changes ship at release phase boundaries. Model changes at most once a quarter unless a retirement, a price step or a P0 forces one. A retired model is never used (D395).

5. Incidents and support

  • Incident runbook (INCIDENT-RUNBOOK.md, to be written by spec 17 criterion 10) covers bad output reaching a customer (switch the surface off by its ceiling variable), a provider outage (fallback chain of spec 12) and a data leak (the existing incident procedure and the ICO clock).
  • Support playbook: find the ledger row and trace, explain in plain words, fix the cause, add a golden case, record it in the weekly note. No credit refund (D359).

7. Reading level

api/app/services/plain_language.py checks AI text addressed to the user for reading ease of at least 60 and sentences of 20 words or fewer on average. A surface below target for two weekly reviews gets a prompt change row.
