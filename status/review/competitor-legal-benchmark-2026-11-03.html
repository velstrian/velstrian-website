What this means for us

  • Cap size. Two rivals (AutogenAI, mytender.io) promise a GBP 250,000 floor. We have no floor, so a small customer gets less than theirs. Raise with counsel whether a modest floor is worth offering.
  • Data protection cap. No rival has a separate data cap. Ours is stricter and a selling point, but check it does not read as a lower cap than their general one.
  • DPA. Only AutogenAI and mytender.io publish a usable one. Publishing ours is ahead of five of seven.
  • Breach notice. Only mytender.io gives hours (24h, 72h). AutogenAI and Tenderbase say "without undue delay". Ours (72h) is in line, though mytender.io's 24h is tighter. Ask counsel whether 72h counted from awareness is enough.
  • Sub-processor notice. Ours (30 days) matches mytender.io and beats AutogenAI (14). Counsel should confirm the right to object and exit.
  • Training. Four state no training, and EA Global's terms say the opposite of its security page. A plain "no training" promise is standard, we match it.
  • Transfers and location. Most rivals are vague, though mytender.io states UK hosting and AutogenAI offers regions. Ours is stated: primary data in the EU, US AI providers covered by the UK IDTA or EU SCCs with the UK Addendum. Ask counsel to confirm that basis per provider (question 6 of the counsel email).
  • Pattern. The smaller tools (BidScript, TenderBoard, Tenderbase) publish little, so clear terms are a point of difference for buyers.
