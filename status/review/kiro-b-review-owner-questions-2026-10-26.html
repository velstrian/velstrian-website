Question 1 (B-02). Three liability caps that do not reconcile

Terms section 6 says (1) general cap: the Annual Fees, (2) data protection and security cap: twice the Annual Fees, never more than GBP 250,000, replacing the general cap for those claims, and (3) in the full clause text only: "In any 12 month period our total liability for all claims together will not be more than twice the Annual Fees". The summary card and the table do not mention (3).

The conflict: (3) can let general claims reach twice the Annual Fees once a data claim exists, and it can cut a data claim below GBP 250,000 when two claims fall in one year. A reader cannot tell which wins.

Option One reconciling sentence Effect
A (recommended) "In any 12 month period our total liability for all claims together, of every kind, will not be more than twice the Annual Fees and never more than GBP 250,000, and within that total, claims other than data protection and security claims will not be more than the Annual Fees." Keeps all three numbers, makes one overall ceiling and puts the general cap inside it
B Delete sentence (3) Two caps only, each per claim set. Simplest, but no overall annual ceiling
C Keep (3) and add it to the card and table as a third row Shows the reader all three, leaves the overlap for counsel

Recommended: A, then counsel confirms it (A-02). The figures stay the owner's. Card, table and clause are made consistent only after your answer.

Question 3 (B-12). "Data Protection Officer"

The Privacy Policy names a "Data Protection Officer" in three places (aside card, section heading, footer ledger) with dpo@velstrian.com. Under UK GDPR Article 37 a formal DPO is only required for large scale monitoring or special category data, and naming one brings Article 38 and 39 duties (independence, reporting to the highest level, no conflict).

Option Effect
A (recommended, unless a DPO is formally appointed) Rename to "data protection contact", keep dpo@velstrian.com working
B Keep "Data Protection Officer" because one is formally appointed, and name the role holder internally

Question: is a formal Article 37 DPO appointed? Answer "B-12 A" or "B-12 B".
