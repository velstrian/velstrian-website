Rules used to fill this table

  1. Status Implemented requires evidence that exists today. A file path, a migration, a configuration value, a live setting or a measurement taken on a named date. No control is marked implemented on the strength of an intention, a design document or a plan.
  2. Partial means the control operates but does not fully meet the control objective, and the shortfall is named in the justification.
  3. Planned means the control does not operate and there is a stated intent to build it.
  4. Not implemented means the control does not operate.
  5. Not applicable is a positive statement that the risk does not apply to this organisation, and every such row carries the reason. It is not shorthand for "we did not get to it".
  6. Where a claim could not be verified, the row says Unverified and names what would verify it.
