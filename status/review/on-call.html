Out of hours detection path

Alert emails reach contact@velstrian.com at any hour. Nobody is paid to read them overnight (D354), so the first human look is the next UK business morning. The path is: Sentry alert email, the owner or driver reads it at the start of the next working day, opens the Sentry issue, then INCIDENT-RUNBOOK.md. Surfaces fail closed meanwhile.

Breach notice clock (R285-LEGAL-KIRO-B04, 2026-10-26). DPA clause 6 (public page) and clause 8.2 (canonical DPA) now say 72 hours from awareness (D459, replacing the 48 hours of D314), and awareness means reasonable certainty that a breach has happened. The 72 hours stays. Worst case on this path: an alert that lands at 18:00 on a Friday is first read at 09:00 on Monday, 63 hours later, which leaves 9 hours to confirm and notify. A bank holiday Monday pushes the first read past 72 hours, so the clock is only safe if someone reads the alert inbox once a day on non working days. Owner and driver steps, no code: (1) invite platform@velstrian.com as a Sentry organisation member so the high priority and AI route alerts reach the platform mailbox as well as contact@velstrian.com (Sentry, Settings, Members, Invite), (2) turn on the Sentry mobile app push for those rules, (3) agree who glances at the alert inbox once on each non working day. Recorded as an owner step on the approval page, not done here.
