Top 5 gaps, with recommended wording

All drafts are British English, positive and short, for owner approval then counsel.

1. Business only sale (topic 11), clause for section 1 or 2. "Velstrian is sold to businesses and other organisations for use in their trade, business or profession. By signing up you confirm you are buying for that purpose."

2. No warranty for the service (topic 2), clause for section 8. "We work hard to keep Velstrian running well and we fix problems quickly. The service is provided as it is and as available. We do not promise it will always be uninterrupted or error free, and we do not give any warranty the law lets us leave out."

3. Bid outcome not guaranteed (topic 7), clause for section 4. "Velstrian helps you write better bids, faster. Whether a bid wins is decided by the buyer, so we cannot promise any result."

4. Refund and AI accuracy (topic 12), clause for section 2. "Because you review every AI output before you use it, we do not refund or credit fees because an output is inaccurate or incomplete. We will still look into any answer you think is wrong and help put it right."

5. Export and sanctions (topic 9), clause for section 3. "You confirm that you and your users are not on a UK, US or EU sanctions list and are not in a country under a trade embargo, and you will not use Velstrian in breach of export or sanctions law."

Also worth one line each at the same pass: topic 5 (name both AI providers in terms.md:87 and add "We use trusted AI providers, and a change at one of them can affect the service") and topic 10 (refresh section 3 to mention bids and add "do not misuse the AI, for example by trying to extract its instructions or build a competing product").
