# SEO audit, velstrian.com, 2026-10-28 (quick scope)

Scope was cut by the coordinator mid run. Measured with curl only, no browser, no Lighthouse. Corpus: all 96 sitemap URLs fetched once (all returned 200 with no redirect, landed URL equal to listed URL), 4 pages read in detail. Internal link crawl was NOT done.

## Verdict
Strong technical SEO. No P0. The weak spots are the www host (no redirect to apex), title length on help pages, and a few short titles.

## Gaps
P1 www host serves 200, no redirect. Evidence: https://www.velstrian.com/about/ returns 200 with no hops. The canonical points to the apex, so duplicate risk is reduced but not removed. Fix: one Cloudflare redirect rule www to apex (a free plan setting, owner decision).

P2 41 of 96 titles are over 60 characters (mostly /help/ pages, up to 91 chars, e.g. /help/buyer-award-standstill-and-letters/ at 89). Google will cut them in results. Fix: shorten and drop the "| Velstrian help" suffix on long ones.

P2 16 titles are under 30 characters, e.g. /about/ "About Us | Velstrian" (20), /security/, /partners/, /terms/. Weak keywords. Fix: add a plain phrase, e.g. "About Velstrian, bid software for small firms".

P2 28 /help/ pages are under 300 words (lowest 139, /help/account-security-and-data/). Fine for help, but thin for search. Decide whether to noindex help stubs or enrich them.

P3 21 meta descriptions run over 160 characters (up to 247 on /blog/life-after-award-kpis-contract-performance/). They will be truncated.

P3 /ABOUT/ (upper case) returns 404, which is correct. /about without slash returns 308 to /about/, consistent trailing slash.

P3 Photo images: 67 of 118 img tags are jpg (blog photos), the rest webp. Convert blog jpg to webp. All have alt and width, 14 not lazy (likely hero).

P3 Never words. "council" appears in running text on 11 pages (/bid/, /buy/, /sectors/*, etc). Owner rule is that Buyer is never called "council". Check each use is a generic example of a buyer type and not a product name. "PPN 002" appears on /blog/ppn-026-what-has-published-and-what-has-not/ in a section titled "Where PPN 002 stands". This is explanatory text, but the owner locked PPN 026 only. Owner decision whether to keep the section.
