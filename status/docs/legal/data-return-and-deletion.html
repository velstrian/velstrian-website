This policy explains how a Customer exports (returns) its data, how long Velstrian retains data, and how and when data is deleted — including the right to erasure under Article 17 and the right to data portability under Article 20. It supports and is referenced by the Data Processing Agreement (docs/legal/data-processing-agreement.md, clause 9) and applies to Velstrian Bid and Velstrian Buy.

This policy is grounded in the platform's actual, shipped export and deletion mechanisms, cited below so the commitments match what the software does.

1. How the Customer exports (returns) its data

1.1 Self-service in-product export (Art. 20 portability). Authenticated users can export their account and organisation data on demand. The export is produced as a ZIP archive (application/zip) containing one JSON file per data table plus a manifest.json and a README.txt, and is downloaded directly over TLS.

  • Mechanism: GET /v1/user/export → export_data (api/app/routers/user.py:286), which calls build_gdpr_zip (api/app/services/account_deletion.py:404). The download filename is crowagent-gdpr-export-<timestamp>.zip.
  • Web surfaces: web/app/api/account/export, web/app/api/user/data-export, and web/app/api/account/export/request / export/list for the asynchronous DSAR pipeline.
  • Coverage. The archive covers the user's profile and organisation records plus the product content tables — assets/properties, compliance results, reports, monitoring alerts, notifications, Velstrian Bid contracts / measures / narratives / missions, evidence items and log, subscriptions, activity log, AI-usage records, API-key metadata, and NPS responses (see the _EXPORT_TABLES list at api/app/services/account_deletion.py:311). A manifest.json records the schema version and per-table row counts.
  • Secrets are excluded. Raw API-key values are never included — only key metadata is exported (api/app/services/account_deletion.py:533).
  • Completeness guarantee. A read failure on any table aborts the whole export rather than silently delivering a partial archive (GdprExportError, api/app/services/account_deletion.py:518-530), so a Subject Access export is complete or it fails loudly.

1.2 Sensitive-action protection. Because a full export is a bulk-PII operation, the endpoint requires an MFA (AAL2) step-up where the user has MFA enrolled (require_aal2_for_sensitive_action, api/app/routers/user.py:295) and is rate-limited to 5 exports per day (api/app/routers/user.py:287).

1.3 Encrypted asynchronous (DSAR) export. For subject-access requests fulfilled asynchronously, the archive is written to storage AES-256 encrypted (pyzipper) with a fresh, high-entropy CSPRNG passphrase (generate_export_passphrase, api/app/services/account_deletion.py:38) that is delivered out of band in the export-ready email — the passphrase never appears in the object key, filename, or signed URL.

1.4 Formats. Structured data is exported as JSON (per the mechanism above); generated documents/reports are available in their native product format (e.g. PDF, and DOCX where a product offers branded document export). [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] JSON is the canonical structured-export format, additionally offered as CSV where a product already exposes it (e.g. web/app/api/core/export-csv, web/app/api/properties/export-csv); confirm this canonical customer-facing format list.

1.5 Assisted / bulk export. Where a bulk or full-account export exceeds the self-service feature, the Customer may request an assisted export by emailing contact@velstrian.com; Velstrian will provide it within [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 14 days of a verified request.

2. Return window on termination

2.1 On termination or expiry, the Customer may export or request return of its data for a period of [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30 days from the effective termination date (the "Return Window"). During the Return Window the account is retained in a read-only / retrieval state to allow export via the mechanisms in clause 1.

2.2 The Customer is responsible for completing its exports within the Return Window. Velstrian will give reasonable notice of the Return Window's start and end.

2.3 On request during the Return Window, Velstrian will assist with return of the data in the formats set out in clause 1.4.

3. Deletion timeline and mechanism (Art. 17 erasure)

3.1 Two deletion paths ship in the platform:

  • (a) Grace-scheduled deletion (default self-service). When a user requests account deletion, the account is soft-deleted immediately (the user is locked out) and a hard delete is scheduled 14 days later, giving a grace/undo window. The user receives an email confirming the scheduled erasure date with a cancel link, and can cancel any time within the 14 days.

    • Mechanism: enqueue_deletion_request writes a deletion_requests row with scheduled_for = now + 14 days and sets profiles.deleted_at = NOW() (api/app/services/deletion_service.py:317, grace constant _GRACE_PERIOD_DAYS = 14 at :60-61). Cancellation via cancel_deletion_request (:397) restores the profile. A scheduler, process_due_deletion_requests (:694), hard-deletes rows whose scheduled_for has elapsed.
    • Web surfaces: web/app/api/account/delete/request, web/app/api/account/delete/cancel, web/app/api/account/cancel-deletion, and the completion email cron web/app/api/cron/account-deleted-emails.

  • (b) Immediate deletion. A direct, no-grace erasure path that runs the destructive cascade synchronously after re-authentication.

    • Mechanism: POST /v1/user/delete → delete_account (api/app/routers/user.py:49). It requires a password reconfirmation (api/app/routers/user.py:91-111) and an MFA (AAL2) step-up (:57), is rate-limited to 3/hour (:50), cancels Stripe subscriptions before the database delete (:119), runs the cascade, deletes the auth user, revokes tokens, and emails a confirmation.

3.2 Hard-delete mechanics (both paths). Erasure runs the SECURITY DEFINER atomic_delete_user RPC in a single transaction, cascading all org-scoped and user-scoped rows (api/app/services/deletion_service.py:506; api/app/routers/user.py:152), then deletes the auth.users row, globally revokes refresh tokens, and records still-unexpired access tokens in revoked_tokens so the request proxy refuses them for the remainder of their short lifetime (api/app/routers/user.py:198-235; api/app/services/token_revocation.py).

3.3 Active-system deletion timing. Path (b) deletes from active production systems immediately; path (a) does so at the end of the 14-day grace window. In all cases active-system deletion completes no later than [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30 days after the grace window / a verified deletion instruction.

3.4 Audit-log handling (Art. 17 vs Art. 5(1)(d) accuracy). Velstrian retains a minimal, anonymised audit record of security-relevant actions. On erasure, personally identifying information is scrubbed from the immutable audit_log via the anonymise_audit_log_for_user RPC (api/app/services/deletion_service.py:472); only organisation reference, action and timestamp are retained, for up to [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 7 years (per the platform code's current retention setting). The final erasure event itself is recorded against a one-way salted pseudonym, never the raw user identifier (_deletion_subject_pseudonym, api/app/services/deletion_service.py:90), so the erased identity is not re-introduced into the ledger. If anonymisation cannot be completed, the request is honestly marked completed_with_warnings and the confirmation email does not claim the audit PII was removed (api/app/services/deletion_service.py:259-314).

4. Backup handling and purge lag

4.1 After active-system deletion, Customer data may persist in encrypted, access-controlled backups and will be purged on the normal backup-rotation cycle, no later than [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30–90 days after active-system deletion.

4.2 During that window the backup data is retained solely for disaster recovery, is not used for any other purpose, and is protected by the security measures in the DPA. If a backup containing deleted data is restored for recovery, Velstrian re-applies the deletion.

4.3 Sub-processor deletion. Velstrian instructs its Sub-Processors (see the DPA Schedule 1) to delete Customer data in line with this policy, subject to each Sub-Processor's own backup cycles. Stripe subscription data is cancelled/removed as part of the deletion flow (clause 3.1(b)); Stripe retains billing records it is required to keep as a payments provider.

5. Legal-retention exceptions

5.1 Velstrian may retain limited data beyond the periods above where required by UK/EU law or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, for example:

  • billing, invoicing and tax records to meet UK statutory accounting/tax obligations — 7 years (per HMRC / Companies Act norms, owner decision D314, 2026-10-23, the same period as the public retention page and the DPA clause 9.2); and
  • minimal, anonymised security and audit-log entries — [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] up to 7 years (per the platform code default; see clause 3.4).

5.2 Any retained data is minimised, isolated, access-restricted, and deleted once the legal basis for retention ends.

6. Certification of deletion

6.1 On the Customer's written request, Velstrian will provide written certification that Customer data has been deleted in accordance with this policy, confirming the date of active-system deletion and the expected/actual date of backup purge.

6.2 Certification is provided within [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30 days of a verified request to contact@velstrian.com.
