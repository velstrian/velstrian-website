1. Uptime commitment

1.1 Velstrian will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services Available for at least the Uptime Commitment below in each calendar month, measured under clause 2 and excluding the events in clause 3:

Plan tier Monthly Uptime Commitment
Standard (all paid plans by default) [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 99.5% / month
Enterprise (where expressly agreed) [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 99.9% / month

Context for the owner's decision (not a contractual term):

  • 99.5%/month ≈ up to ~3h 39m of allowed downtime per 30-day month — a common, defensible SME starting point.
  • 99.9%/month ≈ up to ~43m 50s of allowed downtime per 30-day month — harder to meet on a small infrastructure footprint and increases service-credit exposure.

1.2 "Available" means the core application responds to authenticated requests and serves its primary functions. A degradation limited to a single non-core feature, or to an optional integration provided by a third party, is not treated as unavailability of the whole Service.

2. Measurement

2.1 Measurement window. Uptime is measured per calendar month (UTC).

2.2 Monthly Uptime Percentage = ((Total minutes in month − Excluded minutes − Downtime minutes) ÷ (Total minutes in month − Excluded minutes)) × 100, where "Downtime minutes" are minutes during which the Service was not Available, excluding the events in clause 3.

2.3 Source of truth. Availability is measured by Velstrian Tenderworks' monitoring and reflected on the public status page at https://status.velstrian.com (reached from https://app.crowagent.ai/status, which redirects there). Monitoring probes the app (https://app.crowagent.ai), the marketing site (https://crowagent.ai) and the API health endpoint (https://api.crowagent.ai/healthz) at 5-minute intervals and raises an incident after 2 consecutive failed checks (≈10 minutes). Customers may reference the status page for incident history (uptime shown over the last 90 days).

2.4 Downtime start/stop. An incident begins when the Service is first confirmed unavailable (by monitoring or a verified Customer report) and ends when service is restored.

3. Exclusions

The following do not count as Downtime and are excluded from the Uptime calculation:

3.1 Scheduled maintenance for which Velstrian gives at least [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 48 hours advance notice via the status page and/or email, scheduled where practicable during a low-usage window ([DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 22:00–06:00 UK time, weekends).

3.2 Emergency maintenance reasonably required to address a security vulnerability or imminent risk to the Service, undertaken with as much notice as is practicable. (Velstrian operates a production-only deployment model; urgent security patches may be deployed with minimal notice.)

3.3 Force majeure — events beyond Velstrian Tenderworks' reasonable control (internet backbone failures, DDoS attacks, acts of government, natural disasters, widespread cloud-provider outages).

3.4 Third-party / Sub-Processor outages outside Velstrian Tenderworks' reasonable control, including failures of the underlying hosting (Vercel, Railway), database (Supabase), CDN/WAF (Cloudflare), email (Brevo), payments (Stripe), AI providers (Anthropic, Google), or analytics (PostHog), and outages of optional Customer-connected integrations (e.g. Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Companies House, EPC register).

3.5 Customer-caused issues — the Customer's own network or equipment; misuse or use contrary to the documentation or acceptable-use terms; the Customer's own configuration changes; suspension for non-payment; or exceeding published rate/usage limits (e.g. the export/deletion rate limits).

3.6 Beta, trial, free-tier, and clearly-labelled experimental or "coming soon" features.

5. Service credits

5.1 If the Monthly Uptime Percentage for a Service falls below the applicable Uptime Commitment in a calendar month, the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy is a service credit against a future invoice, on request, per the schedule below.

5.2 Service-credit schedule (percentage of the affected monthly subscription fee for the affected Service):

Monthly Uptime Percentage Service credit
Below Commitment but ≥ 99.0% [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 5%
≥ 95.0% and < 99.0% [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 10%
< 95.0% [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 25%

5.3 Claim process. To receive a credit the Customer must submit a request to contact@velstrian.com within [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30 days of the end of the affected month, including the dates/times of unavailability. Velstrian validates the claim against its monitoring and status-page records.

5.4 Cap. Total service credits in any single month will not exceed [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 100% of that month's fee for the affected Service.

5.5 Service credits are non-refundable, have no cash value, apply only to future invoices, and are not available while the Customer's account is in arrears.

6. Escalation path

6.1 Standard route: raise a ticket at contact@velstrian.com with the severity, impact, and any error detail. For a suspected outage, check https://status.velstrian.com first — an active incident means Velstrian is already engaged.

6.2 If an S1 issue is not acknowledged within the S1 target in clause 4.3, or the Customer is dissatisfied with progress, the Customer may escalate to [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] the Velstrian operations owner at contact@velstrian.com.

6.3 Velstrian will provide status updates on an active S1/S2 incident at reasonable intervals (target: roughly every [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30–60 minutes for S1) via the ticket and/or the status page until resolution.
