3. Every AI call site, by surface

One entry point. All chat and embedding calls in the API go through AIClient. The provider is chosen from the model name inside the provider layer. No other module imports an AI vendor SDK except gemini_rag.py, which builds its own genai.Client at startup (gemini_rag.py:18, :1025) but sends nothing through it, and routers/health.py:515, which lists Gemini models with the key as a liveness probe and carries no customer text.

Bypasses found (a finding in itself):

  1. gemini_rag.py keeps a second, unused-for-sending Gemini client object. Its own comment (lines 1028-1035) says the accessor was deleted. Risk is low but a future caller could use it and skip the ledger. Recommendation: remove the second client so the provider layer is the only holder.
  2. routers/health.py:515 calls the Google models list endpoint directly with the key. No customer data, but it is a direct vendor call outside AIClient.
  3. The founders portal (apps/portal/src/lib/ai/client.ts, claude-client.ts) has its own TypeScript AIClient to Anthropic. client.ts:537-543 refuses Gemini surfaces there. That is a second client, not a bypass of policy, but it is outside the Python ledger path. Portal is internal only.
  4. No OpenAI, Cohere, Mistral or similar import exists in api/app. The six files that matched a loose word search (bid_ocr.py, chunking.py, gemini_response.py, truncation.py, bid_answer_marker.py, bid_requirement_extractor.py) were not opened beyond bid_ocr.py, so a hit in the other five is unconfirmed as prose.

Anthropic Claude API

  • Training: "By default, we will not use your inputs or outputs from our commercial products" to train models. Exceptions are feedback or bug reports submitted to Anthropic, and explicit opt in. Source: https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training, read 2026-10-22.
  • Retention: "we automatically delete inputs and outputs on our backend within 30 days of receipt or generation". Flagged usage policy violations up to 2 years, classification scores up to 7 years. Zero data retention applies "when you and we have agreed otherwise". Page dated July 1, 2026. Source: https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data, read 2026-10-22.
  • Region: inference_geo accepts global (default) or us. Workspace geo is us only. US only inference is priced at 1.1 times. The parameter is supported on Claude 4.6 and later, and returns a 400 on Haiku 4.5. Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/data-residency (canonical manage-claude/data-residency), read 2026-10-22.
