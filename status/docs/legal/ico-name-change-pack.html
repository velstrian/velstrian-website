ICO registration name change pack

Row: R285-REBRAND-13-REGISTRATIONS-AND-LISTINGS. Date: 2026-10-23. Registration reference: C1901492. New legal name: Velstrian Systems Ltd (Companies House number 17076461, name change confirmed done by the owner on 2026-10-23). Trading name: Velstrian Tenderworks. Contact: contact@velstrian.com.

1. The answer

A change of legal name cannot be done online. The ICO's "Change or update your details" page covers organisation address, contact details, DPO details, trading names and payment tier, and says to change the organisation name "please call our helpline on 0303 123 1113 or contact us by email". Source: ICO, change or update your details (read 2026-10-23).

What the page asks for:

  • By phone: "your registration reference and security number".
  • By email: "email your request to us quoting your registration reference". If you have no security number, contact by email. The email address sits behind a protected link on the page, so open the page and use the address shown there (it was not readable as plain text when checked).
  • The page does not say whether a Companies House change of name certificate is needed. So have it to hand as a precaution, but it is not stated as a requirement.

The trading name can be added online (up to five trading names) by the nominated contact on the same page. Do that after the legal name is changed, so the public register shows the new legal name with Velstrian Tenderworks as the trading name.

Also noted on the ICO news page: the ICO was due to become the Information Commission on 30 September 2026, and says it will still be known as the ICO (ICO news). The search did not show whether this changes the registration process, so use the page and the helpline number above as they stand and ask on the call if anything sounds different.

3. Short call script

Call 0303 123 1113 (the ICO registration helpline). Say:

"Hello. I am calling to change the organisation name on a data protection fee registration. Our registration reference is C1901492. Our company has changed its legal name at Companies House, and I need the name on the register changed to Velstrian Systems Ltd. The Companies House number is 17076461 and it stays the same company, so it is a name change and not a new organisation."

If asked for the security number, read it out. If asked for proof, say you can email the Companies House confirmation straight away.

Ask before ending the call:

  1. "Does the registration number stay the same?" (expected yes, confirm)
  2. "Will the public register show the new name, and by when?"
  3. "Does the fee, renewal date or Direct Debit change in any way?"
  4. "Should I send the Companies House paperwork by email, and to which address?"
  5. "Can I add Velstrian Tenderworks as a trading name online afterwards?"
  6. "Please send written confirmation to contact@velstrian.com."

Write down the date, the name of the person, and any case number.

4. Email fallback (if the owner prefers email to a call)

Send from contact@velstrian.com to the address on the ICO page.

Subject: Organisation name change, registration C1901492

Hello,

Please change the organisation name on data protection fee registration C1901492 to Velstrian Systems Ltd (Companies House number 17076461). The company has changed its name at Companies House and it is the same company. The Companies House confirmation is attached.

Registered office: Reading, Berkshire, RG1 6SP. We will add Velstrian Tenderworks as a trading name online once the legal name is updated. Please confirm by reply when the register is updated, and whether anything else is needed from us.

Velstrian Systems Ltd contact@velstrian.com

Note: the ICO says to include the registration reference. If a security number is held, quote it in the email only if the ICO page asks for it. Do not paste it anywhere shared.

5. After the change

  1. Search the ICO register for C1901492 and confirm the new name shows (the page tells users the business details are published on the public register).
  2. Online, add the trading name Velstrian Tenderworks (nominated contact signs in).
  3. Check the next renewal reminder carries the new name.
  4. Update the row R285-REBRAND-13-REGISTRATIONS-AND-LISTINGS to say the ICO entry is done, with the date and the case number.
  5. The DPA and Terms already carry C1901492 and the new name, so no wording change is needed there.

6. Cost and effort

No fee for a name change is stated on the ICO page. The data protection fee itself is not changed by a name change (ask on the call, question 3). Effort: about 10 minutes on the phone or 5 minutes to send the email.
