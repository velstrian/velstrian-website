0.1 Definitions

In this DPA:

  • "Customer Personal Data" (equivalently "Controller Personal Data") means Personal Data submitted to, or generated by, the Services by or on behalf of the Customer.
  • "Data Protection Legislation" means the UK GDPR (as defined in the Data Protection Act 2018), the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), and, where the Customer or relevant Data Subjects are in the EEA, the EU GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), together with any successor or replacement legislation.
  • "Services" / "Platform" means the Velstrian software-as-a-service products described in clause 0 and the Agreement.
  • "ICO" means the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • "Processing", "Data Subject", "Personal Data", "Supervisory Authority", "Sub-Processor" and other capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings given in the UK GDPR / EU GDPR as applicable.

1. Roles of the parties

1.1 In respect of Customer Personal Data submitted to, or generated by, the Services, the Customer is the Controller and Velstrian is the Processor.

1.2 Where Velstrian engages a third party to process Customer Personal Data on its behalf, that third party acts as a Sub-Processor (Schedule 1). Velstrian remains fully liable to the Customer for the acts and omissions of its Sub-Processors.

1.3 Velstrian acts as an independent Controller only for the limited data it processes for its own business administration — for example account and billing records, its own security and audit logs, and the identity of the Customer's authorised users for authentication. That processing is governed by Velstrian Tenderworks' Privacy Notice, not by this DPA.

2. Subject-matter, duration, nature and purpose (Art. 28(3))

2.1 Subject-matter. Velstrian Tenderworks' processing of Customer Personal Data in order to provide the Services.

2.2 Duration. For the term of the Agreement, plus the return and deletion periods set out in the Data Return and Deletion Policy (docs/legal/data-return-and-deletion-policy.md) and in clause 9.

2.3 Nature and purpose. Provision of UK bid and tender SaaS for suppliers (Velstrian Bid) and buyers (Velstrian Buy), including: authentication and account management; storage of Customer records; document, report and evidence generation; AI-assisted drafting and analysis; subscription billing; transactional email; product analytics and error monitoring; and, where the Customer chooses to enable them, optional integrations with CRM and public-data sources.

2.4 The detailed categories of Personal Data and Data Subjects are set out in Schedule 2.

3. Customer (Controller) instructions

3.1 Velstrian processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by UK or EU law (in which case Velstrian will inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits such notice on important grounds of public interest).

3.2 This DPA, the Agreement, and the Customer's configuration and use of the Services' features together constitute the Customer's documented instructions. Additional or different instructions must be agreed in writing and may be subject to a change in the Agreement or fees.

3.3 If Velstrian believes an instruction infringes UK GDPR, EU GDPR or other applicable data protection law, it will inform the Customer without undue delay.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 Velstrian ensures that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by an appropriate obligation of confidentiality (contractual or statutory) and are trained on their data-protection responsibilities.

4.2 Access to Customer Personal Data is restricted to personnel who need it to provide, secure or support the Services (least-privilege).

5. Security of processing (Art. 32)

5.1 Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing as well as the risk to Data Subjects, Velstrian implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

5.2 Current technical and organisational measures implemented in the platform include:

  • Encryption in transit — all client and inter-service traffic is served over TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest — Customer data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase, EU region) with encryption at rest; stored third-party integration credentials are additionally encrypted at the application layer.
  • Tenant isolation — organisation-level row-level security (RLS) is enforced on tenant tables so one organisation cannot read another's data; the foreign key organisation_id scopes Customer data throughout.
  • Authentication and step-up — password and OAuth sign-in via Supabase Auth, with multi-factor authentication (MFA / AAL2) and a server-side AAL2 step-up requirement for sensitive actions such as account deletion and bulk data export (api/app/dependencies/auth.py require_aal2_for_sensitive_action, applied at api/app/routers/user.py:57 and api/app/routers/user.py:295).
  • Token revocation — on account deletion, refresh tokens are globally invalidated and still-unexpired access tokens are recorded in a revoked_tokens table that the request proxy checks on the hot path (api/app/services/token_revocation.py; enforced in web/proxy.ts).
  • Rate limiting — sensitive endpoints are throttled (e.g. account deletion 3/hour, GDPR export 5/day; see api/app/routers/user.py:50 and :287).
  • Immutable audit logging — security-relevant actions are written to an append-only audit_log with tamper-resistance triggers.
  • Error and performance monitoring — Sentry (see clause 7 / Schedule 1 for the transfer basis).
  • Secure SDLC / deployment — least-privilege service credentials, secrets held in the hosting provider's encrypted environment store (not in source control), and production-only deployment controls.

5.3 A fuller and current description of measures is maintained in Velstrian Tenderworks' security documentation. 6. Sub-processing (Art. 28(2), 28(4))

6.1 The Customer provides a general written authorisation for Velstrian to engage the Sub-Processors listed in Schedule 1, and to add or replace Sub-Processors subject to the notice process in clause 6.4.

6.2 Velstrian imposes on each Sub-Processor, by written contract, data-protection obligations that are no less protective than those in this DPA, in particular the security obligations in clause 5 and the transfer obligations in clause 7.

6.3 Velstrian remains fully liable to the Customer for a Sub-Processor's performance of its data-protection obligations.

6.4 Change notification. Velstrian will notify the Customer of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-Processor at least [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 30 days before that Sub-Processor begins processing Customer Personal Data, by [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] email to the Customer's account administrator and/or an updated public sub-processor page that Customers may subscribe to. If the Customer reasonably objects on legitimate data-protection grounds within [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] 14 days of the notice, the parties will discuss in good faith; if the objection cannot be resolved, the Customer may terminate the affected Service and receive a pro-rata refund of any pre-paid, unused fees for that Service.

7. International transfers (Chapter V)

7.1 Where Velstrian or a Sub-Processor transfers Customer Personal Data outside the UK (or, for EU GDPR data, outside the EEA), Velstrian ensures an appropriate transfer safeguard is in place, being one of:

  • (a) an adequacy decision/regulation covering the destination; or
  • (b) the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) supplemented by the UK Addendum, together with a transfer risk assessment where required; and, where applicable, reliance on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for a certified US Sub-Processor.

7.2 The primary data store (Supabase, eu-west-1, Ireland) and product analytics (PostHog, EU) are configured in EU regions, and transactional email (Brevo) is an EU provider; for these, the intention is that no restricted transfer arises on location, subject to owner confirmation of the exact region configured for Velstrian's account. Google Gemini is NOT region pinned: the platform calls the Gemini Developer API with an API key and sets no region (api/app/services/ai/provider_calls.py), and Google's terms make no residency commitment, so Gemini is treated as a restricted transfer under clause 7.3, not as EU/UK processing. 8. Assistance to the Controller (Arts. 28(3)(e)–(f), 32–36)

8.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it, Velstrian assists the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures with:

  • (a) responding to Data Subject rights requests — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection — supported in-product by the GDPR export and delete features described in the Data Return and Deletion Policy;
  • (b) security of processing (Art. 32);
  • (c) personal data breach notification and communication (Arts. 33–34); and
  • (d) data protection impact assessments and prior consultation (Arts. 35–36).

8.2 Breach notification. Velstrian will notify the Customer without undue delay and, in any event, within 48 hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data (owner decision D314, 2026-10-23), and will provide the information the Customer reasonably needs to meet its own Art. 33/34 obligations, including (to the extent known) the nature of the breach, likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

9. Return and deletion on termination (Art. 28(3)(g))

9.1 On termination or expiry of the Services, at the Customer's choice, Velstrian will return or delete Customer Personal Data in accordance with the Data Return and Deletion Policy (docs/legal/data-return-and-deletion-policy.md), including the return window, active-system deletion timeline, and backup-purge lag described there.

9.2 Velstrian may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent required by UK or EU law, and will keep any such data minimised, isolated and protected by the clause 5 measures for the remaining period, deleting it once the legal basis for retention ends. Billing, invoicing and tax records are kept for 7 years to meet HMRC and Companies Act obligations (owner decision D314, 2026-10-23), the same period the public retention page states.

9.3 On the Customer's written request, Velstrian will certify deletion in accordance with the Data Return and Deletion Policy.

10. Audit (Art. 28(3)(h))

10.1 Velstrian will make available to the Customer the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with the Art. 28 obligations, and will allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor it mandates.

10.2 For an SME context, audit rights are ordinarily satisfied by Velstrian providing its security documentation and responses to a reasonable due-diligence questionnaire. On-site or Sub-Processor audits are limited to [DEFAULT — owner to confirm] once per 12 months, on reasonable notice (default 30 days), at the Customer's cost, during normal business hours without undue disruption, and subject to confidentiality — or where required by a supervisory authority.

11. Liability and term

Owner decision D314 (2026-10-23): liability option B, insurance backed. Two separate caps, the same as section 6 of the Terms of Service. Still subject to counsel review (see the sign off pack).

11.1 General cap. For general commercial breach, including defects in the Services (bugs) and service interruptions, Velstrian's aggregate liability under or in connection with the Agreement and this DPA shall not exceed the greater of (a) the total subscription fees paid by the Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim, or (b) £2,500.

11.2 Data protection and security cap. For data protection and security breaches under this DPA, including any personal data breach, a separate cap applies, distinct from the cap in clause 11.1. Velstrian's aggregate liability for those claims shall not exceed the lesser of (a) £250,000, or (b) the actual proceeds recovered under Velstrian's Cyber / Technology Professional Indemnity insurance policy for that claim.

11.3 Excluded loss. Velstrian shall not be liable for any indirect loss, consequential loss, loss of profits or loss of business, however arising.

11.4 No indemnity for fines. Velstrian gives no indemnity to the Customer for regulatory fines or penalties imposed on the Customer by any supervisory authority (for example the ICO).

11.5 What is never limited. Nothing in this DPA or the Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability that cannot be limited or excluded by law.

11.6 Term. This DPA takes effect on the effective date of the Agreement and continues while Velstrian processes Customer Personal Data. Clauses that by their nature should survive termination (including clauses 4, 5, 7, 9, 10 and 11) survive.

Schedule 2 — Details of Processing

Categories of Data Subjects: the Customer's authorised users and staff; the Customer's own clients, contacts and counterparties whose data the Customer chooses to enter; and other individuals referenced in documents the Customer uploads or generates.

Categories of Personal Data:

  • Identity and contact data (name, email, job title, organisation).
