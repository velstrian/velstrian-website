Disclaimer Text (for in-product display and ToS)

AI-Generated Content Notice

The outputs produced by Velstrian (including EPC compliance assessments, MEES guidance, PPN 002 Social Value scores, recommendations, and any other AI-generated content) are generated by artificial intelligence models and are provided for informational and decision-support purposes only.

These outputs do not constitute legal advice, surveying advice, financial advice, energy performance certification, or any other form of professional advice or regulated activity. Nothing in the outputs should be relied upon as a substitute for advice from a qualified professional such as a chartered surveyor, accredited energy assessor, procurement lawyer, or social value specialist.

Velstrian Systems Ltd makes no representation or warranty (express or implied) as to the accuracy, completeness, currency, or fitness for any particular purpose of AI-generated outputs. AI models can produce errors, hallucinations, outdated information, or outputs that are inappropriate for specific circumstances.

You are solely responsible for any decision or action taken in reliance on outputs from Velstrian. Velstrian Systems Ltd accepts no liability for any loss or damage arising from reliance on AI-generated content without independent professional verification.

Solicitor Review Checklist

1. Scope of disclaimer

  • Is the disclaimer sufficiently broad to cover all AI model outputs (EPC, MEES, PPN 002, future features)?
  • Should the disclaimer explicitly reference specific regulated activities (energy certification under the Energy Performance of Buildings Regulations 2012)?
  • Is the disclaimer appropriate for B2B SaaS customers as well as individual users?

2. Consumer Rights Act 2015 considerations

  • For any consumer-facing use, does the limitation of liability clause comply with the CRA 2015 fairness test (Schedule 2)?
  • Is the disclaimer presented prominently enough to be incorporated into the contract at the point of sign-up?

3. Professional liability

  • Does the disclaimer adequately exclude liability for professional advice under the Law Reform (Contributory Negligence) Act 1945?
  • Should we reference any specific professional standards (RICS, CIBSE, Chartered Institute of Procurement & Supply)?

4. FSMA 2000 / FCA regulatory perimeter

  • Do any outputs touch on financial instruments or regulated financial advice that would require FCA authorisation?
  • Confirm: MEES/EPC compliance guidance and PPN 002 scoring are not regulated activities under FSMA 2000.

5. AI Act (EU) / future UK AI regulation

  • Confirm current classification of Velstrian outputs under the proposed UK AI regulatory framework.
  • Note: EU AI Act high-risk classifications for energy performance and public procurement AI systems — confirm applicability if EU customers are served.

6. Placement and prominence

  • Recommend placement: (a) in Terms of Service, (b) inline on each AI output result, (c) in B2B contract DPA?
  • Should the disclaimer be shown as a modal/interstitial on first AI output, or is footer/ToS reference sufficient?
