  • Data protection and security cap (Terms 6, D460 C2). Twice the Annual Fees, never more than £250,000, replacing the general cap for those claims and not adding to it. A higher limit can be agreed for an extra charge. The insurance proceeds formula and floor of D319 are removed.

Never limited (C4) unchanged. Claims window (C8) 12 months from when the customer knew or should have known.
  • Indemnities (C5, C6). The customer covers claims about its content or misuse. No indemnity for AI output. A software infringement claim is met by a fix, a replacement or ending the subscription with a refund.
  • Warranty (C7). Reasonable skill and care, a fix or a refund as the only remedy, 99.5% uptime a target with no credits, otherwise as is. An inaccurate or incomplete AI output is not a failure under it, which keeps it in step with the D459 rule: no refund or credit because an output is inaccurate or incomplete, for every customer.

Assistance beyond the product's own tools chargeable with an estimate first (new 8.3). Sub-processor notice 30 days, sooner only for security or continuity, 14 day objection, refund as the only remedy (6.4, replaces the 7 to 30 days of D358).

1. What exactly is being signed

Two separate things. Both are owner decisions with legal advice.

A. The Data Processing Agreement. File: docs/legal/data-processing-agreement.md, last changed in commit ae2709f29 (D319, before that 9280aada4 for D314). It is the single DPA that Velstrian offers to business customers who ask for an Article 28 contract (the Terms, section 7, tell customers to request it from legal@velstrian.com). It has 12 clauses and three schedules. Schedule 1 lists 17 sub-processors (14 in part A, 3 customer connected in part B). It still carries the DRAFT banner, because the owner review is not finished and counsel has not reviewed it.

What signing means in practice: the owner ratifies the remaining defaults (section 3 below), removes the DRAFT banner after counsel agrees, and from then on Velstrian can send the DPA to customers who ask for it. Customers then countersign (Schedule 3).

B. The liability caps in the Terms. File: crowagent-website/astro/src/content/legal/terms.md, section 6, changed in website commit da6b99d7 (D314) and f48383fd (D319), local only, not deployed. Owner decision D314, option B, insurance backed, with the data protection floor added by D319:

  • General commercial breach, bugs and service interruptions: "the greater of (a) the total subscription fees paid by you in the 12 months preceding the claim, or (b) £2,500" (was £1,000).
  • Data protection and security breaches under the DPA, a separate and distinct cap (D319): "the greater of (a) a floor equal to the greater of the total subscription fees paid by you in the 12 months preceding the claim or £2,500, and (b) the actual proceeds recovered under Velstrian's Cyber / Technology Professional Indemnity insurance policy for that claim, and in any event not more than £250,000". The floor figure is a working default for counsel to set. It is marked [DEFAULT, owner to confirm with counsel] in the DPA and written plainly in the Terms, because the public legal pages carry no draft markers.
  • Indirect loss, consequential loss, loss of profits and loss of business stay excluded (the Terms also keep loss of revenue, loss of data and business interruption, as before).
  • No indemnity to the customer for regulatory fines or penalties imposed by any supervisory authority, for example the ICO.
  • Not limited: death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, anything that cannot be limited by law.

The DPA clause 11 carries the same two caps. The website is not deployed from this pack (RULE 0-G and the website rule: nothing goes to the production website until the owner approves).
