Phase 1. Protect the name

  • How to
    1. Pass a special resolution (a written resolution is enough) saying "That the name of the company be changed to: Velstrian Ltd".
    2. Have your authentication code from step 1.3.
    3. File the change of name online with the resolution attached and pay the fee.
    4. The resolution copy must reach Companies House within 15 days of passing.
    5. Save the new certificate of incorporation when it arrives.

    https://www.gov.uk/make-changes-to-your-limited-company/company-name

  • How to
    1. File it yourself online, no attorney. Filing after step 1.4 lets the applicant be the new company name.
    2. Choose a word mark, standard characters.
    3. Choose classes 9, 35 and 42 and only the goods and services you will sell in the next 5 years. Terms cannot be added later.
    4. Pick Right Start (check first, 28 days to continue) or the standard route, then pay.
    5. Keep the receipt and application number. The six month priority date for filing abroad runs from this day, so put it in your calendar.

    https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark/start-your-application

  • How to
    1. HMRC: update Corporation Tax, PAYE and VAT with the online business route.
    2. ICO: phone 0303 123 1113 with your registration reference, the name cannot be changed online.
    3. Bank: send the new certificate to your relationship manager.
    4. Insurer: ask for the policy schedule to be reissued in the new name.
    • ICO name change pack (rendered)
    • ICO: change your details
    • HMRC business tax account
