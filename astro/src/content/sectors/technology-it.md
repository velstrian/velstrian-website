Public buyers are government departments, councils, the NHS and universities. They buy a lot of technology through frameworks, approved routes that save them running a full tender every time, and they advertise other work on official listing sites. Private buyers, such as companies choosing a software or managed-service supplier, usually send their request for proposal straight to you.

Technology and IT bids are demanding for one reason above all: the security and data-protection questions. Buyers want to see your Cyber Essentials or ISO/IEC 27001 evidence, how you handle personal data, and where it is stored, and they want the same answer every time. Reusing a weak or out-of-date answer costs real work.

Most small and mid size firms have one or two people writing bids between delivery. One place to keep the strongest answers, and to reuse them, is the difference between bidding for everything that fits and only bidding when there is time.
