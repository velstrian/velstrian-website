Public buyers are NHS trusts, integrated care boards and councils buying adult social care, children's services and public health. They advertise on official listing sites, and some NHS work is bought through national frameworks. Private buyers, such as healthcare groups and care operators, usually send their tender straight to you.

Healthcare and care bids turn on quality and safety. Buyers want to see your Care Quality Commission registration and rating where it applies, your safeguarding policies, staff training and checks, and how you handle sensitive data. The same questions recur on nearly every bid, and the evidence has to be exactly right.

Most small and mid size providers write bids between running the service. One place to keep the strongest quality and safeguarding answers, and to reuse them, lets a small team bid for the work that fits without a bid department.
