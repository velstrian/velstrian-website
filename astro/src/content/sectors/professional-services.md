Public buyers are government departments, councils, the NHS and universities. They buy a lot of consultancy through frameworks, approved routes that save them running a full tender every time, and they advertise other work on official listing sites. Private buyers usually send their request for proposal straight to you.

Professional services bids turn on three things: your method, your people and your track record. Buyers want method statements, named people and their CVs, relevant case studies and references, and the same questions come up on nearly every bid. Reusing a weak or out-of-date answer, or missing a deadline, costs real work.

Most small and mid size firms write bids between client work. One place to keep the strongest answers, and to reuse them, is what lets a small team bid for everything that fits.
