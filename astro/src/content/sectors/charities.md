Charities, voluntary organisations and community groups, often called the voluntary, community and social enterprise sector (VCSE), win work two ways. Public contracts are advertised on official listing sites and bought under procurement rules. Grants are where a funder gives money for an agreed purpose and set of outcomes. The questions overlap a great deal, so the same evidence serves both.

What makes this hard is the number of funders and the lean teams. The same questions, about your objects, governance, safeguarding, outcomes and finances, come up on nearly every application, and the person writing the bid is usually also running the service. Reusing a weak or out-of-date answer, or missing a deadline, costs work your community needs.

Velstrian Bid gives one place to find the opportunities, write the application from your own documents, and track the outcomes you promised, which matters most once a grant is awarded.
