<h2 id="where-bid-loses">Where Velstrian Bid loses honestly</h2>
<p>For a pure soft-FM contractor, CleanTender is the more specialised tool. Its vocabulary is tuned to cleaning and security bids, its curated feed filters out the noise of other sectors, and its qualification scan gives a fast bid or no-bid signal from a contract pack in under 30 seconds. Velstrian Bid's advantage is breadth and depth on the compliance side: every sector, a hard figure-grounding gate, social value maths done in code and post-award delivery evidence. If you only ever bid for cleaning contracts, that breadth is not worth much to you, and CleanTender may simply be the better fit. A soft-FM supplier who also bids in other categories could reasonably run both.</p>

<div class="cfaq cmp-longer">
  <details>
    <summary><h3 id="what-is-cleantender">What is CleanTender?</h3></summary>
    <div class="cfaq-a cmp-read">
<p>CleanTender is a UK bid tool built specifically for soft facilities management: cleaning, security, grounds, waste and catering. It curates a feed of soft-FM tenders from official UK public-sector sources, and its qualification scan reads a contract pack and scores your stored profile against it from 0 to 100 in under 30 seconds. On its Pro plan it drafts Selection Questionnaire responses in the standard sections, including Company Overview, Relevant Experience, Quality Assurance, Staff Training, COSHH Management and a Social Value and TOMs section, with the sector vocabulary these bids expect, such as BICSc, SIA ACS, TUPE and NHS national specifications.</p>
<p>That specialisation is a real advantage, and it is one Velstrian Bid does not try to match. If cleaning or security is all you bid for, CleanTender's vocabulary and its fast fit scan are built around exactly your work, and its pricing is public: a free tier to browse the contract directory, and Pro at &pound;99 per month or &pound;990 per year, which adds the qualification scan, unlimited drafts, the Compliance Vault and email alerts. CleanTender positions itself for soft-FM SMEs doing roughly 4 to 12 bids a year.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary><h3 id="what-is-bid">What is Velstrian Bid?</h3></summary>
    <div class="cfaq-a cmp-read">
<p>Velstrian Bid is Velstrian's UK bid and tender management suite, live and ready to use today. It is built for small and mid size firms that write their own bids, for public and private work. Unlike a sector specialist, it is sector-agnostic: it tracks Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Public Contracts Scotland and EU TED across any category, refreshed every morning at 06:00 UTC. It drafts answers grounded in the supplier's own submitted bids, and its defining rule is figure-grounding, so before a draft is shown, every &pound; and % is checked against a set of allowed figures computed from your own data, and if a number is not in that set the draft is rejected. Every AI-assisted draft carries a short note saying AI helped, following the government's PPN 017 guidance, and waits for a person to approve it.</p>
<p>Velstrian Bid calculates social value in code against the weighting the buyer published rather than only drafting it as a questionnaire section, and after award it tracks each commitment to a delivery percentage with a RAG status, captures evidence, issues monthly reports and checks the Procurement Act 2023 KPI duties in sections 52 and 71. Its bid-fit and coverage marking, by contrast, is still in development and not in the released product, so on the fit-scan row above CleanTender is genuinely ahead today.</p>

<p>Velstrian Bid publishes its pricing. Starter is <a href="/pricing/?product=mark">{{bid.starter.monthly}} per month</a> for 1 user, Pro is {{bid.pro.monthly}} per month for 5 users with post-award delivery tracking, and Portfolio is {{bid.portfolio.monthly}} per month for 10 users. Once your account is open you set it up and run it yourself, with no consultant needed, and you pay by card, held securely by Stripe and never stored by us.</p>
