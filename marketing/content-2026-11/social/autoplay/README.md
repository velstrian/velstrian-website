# Autoplay loop posts

12 short looping videos that autoplay well in a feed. Specs here, drafts for review. The clips are recorded by the driver from staging (Option A), then the captions here are burned in.

## Build rules (every loop)

- Length: 6 to 15 seconds, loops cleanly (last frame cuts back to the first).
- Silent first: no sound needed, every word is a burned in caption.
- Sizes: 1080x1350 (feed) and 1080x1920 (stories and reels).
- One idea per loop: a feature in action, a before and after, or a 3 step how to.
- Motion explains, it does not decorate. A short hold on the key frame so the caption can be read.
- Dark theme tokens as for the carousels. Captions in --font-body Inter, --c-text #F7F8FB on a readable scrim over the screen. Accent --c-teal #8C85FF.
- Real product screens from staging, no stock, no person as a customer. No checkout or payment step on screen (P0 open).
- Caption contrast meets WCAG AA. Nothing flashing. Respect reduced motion where the platform offers it.
- British English, no em or en dashes, no semicolons, no hype words. No banned claim. PPN 026 only.
