# Social carousels

12 promotional carousels for LinkedIn and Instagram. Each is a slide by slide spec, drafts for review. Nothing is published or scheduled.

## Build rules (every carousel)

- Sizes: 1080x1350 (portrait) and 1080x1080 (square). Design to the portrait grid, crop safe for square.
- Slides: 6 to 10. Shape: slide 1 the problem, middle slides how Velstrian solves it with a real product screen, last slide one plain call to action.
- One message per slide. Generous space. Strong typography. Motion is not used in a carousel.
- Dark theme. Background --c-bg #090B12, ink --c-text #F7F8FB, support --c-text-sub #D8DAE1, accent --c-teal #8C85FF, label on accent --on-accent #090B12. Fonts --font-display Manrope for headings, --font-body Inter for body. Tokens from velstrian-website/astro/src/styles/tokens.css and brand.source.json (Quantum V, D309).
- Real product screens only, from staging, captured by the driver (same Option A as the videos). No stock photography, no person presented as a customer.
- Contrast meets WCAG AA against the token colours. No flashing.
- British English, sentence case, no em or en dashes, no semicolons, no hype words.
- No win rate, accuracy figure, customer count, testimonial or compliance promise. Social value is PPN 026 only. Data line where used: "Primary data stored in the EU".
- Alt text for every slide image is in social/copy.md.
