# c02 Turn a tender pack into a plan

Product: Velstrian Bid.

- Slide 1 (problem): A tender pack is long, and the clock is running.
- Slide 2 (screen: tender upload): Upload the documents. Velstrian Bid reads them into a clear list of questions.
- Slide 3: A compliance matrix is a checklist of every question, broken into its parts.
- Slide 4 (screen: compliance matrix): Give every part an owner. Nothing slips through.
- Slide 5: Buyer changes during the tender stay in one place, so you always work from the latest version.
- Slide 6 (call to action): Start your next bid from a plan, not a pile. Velstrian Bid.
