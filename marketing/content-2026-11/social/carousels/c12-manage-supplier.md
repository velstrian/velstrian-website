# c12 Manage the supplier after award

Product: Velstrian Buy.

- Slide 1 (problem): The work does not end when the contract is signed.
- Slide 2 (screen: supplier scorecard): Score how a supplier is performing, with a scorecard.
- Slide 3: Every score is kept inside the supplier record, so you know whose it is.
- Slide 4 (screen: supplier review): Hold regular reviews, kept with the same supplier.
- Slide 5 (screen: value tracker): Track what the contract delivers. Totals are kept separate, so a delivered figure is never a forecast.
- Slide 6 (call to action): Keep contracts on track. Velstrian Buy.
