# Social copy

Post text for every carousel and loop, for LinkedIn, X and Instagram, plus hashtags and alt text. Drafts for review. Nothing is scheduled or posted.

Rules: British English, sentence case, no em or en dashes, no semicolons, no hype words. No win rate, accuracy figure, customer count, testimonial or compliance promise. Social value is PPN 026 only. Data line where used: "Primary data stored in the EU". Hashtags kept short and relevant.

Names: Velstrian Tenderworks, Velstrian Bid, Velstrian Buy.
