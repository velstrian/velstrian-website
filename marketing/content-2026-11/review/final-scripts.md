## Script 1: supplier-long (Leda)

1. Writing a bid by yourself is a lot of work.
2. Velstrian Bid helps a small team put a bid together, without needing a bid department.
3. A tender is a public or private buyer asking suppliers to bid for work.
4. Start on the Opportunities screen: one search over Find a Tender, Contracts Finder and Public Contracts Scotland.
5. Each tender gets a relevance score based on your past bids, worked out the same way every time. No AI in the loop.
6. Upload the tender pack and it is read for you, giving you a list of questions to check. Then give each question an owner.
7. Draft from your own documents and past answers, and see which document each point came from.
8. For public work, add your social value answer. Velstrian Bid is built around PPN 026.
9. Review, then export your response as a Word or PDF pack.
10. Velstrian Bid. Bid with confidence, with less of the grind.

## Script 2: buyer-long (Puck)

1. Buying work fairly takes care, whether you are a public body or a private organisation.
2. Velstrian Buy helps you run a clear, consistent process, from requirement to award and supplier reviews.
3. Build a requirement step by step, or start from a template.
4. Your pipeline lists upcoming and current contracts. You can download what your pipeline notice needs, and export a contract register.
5. When bids are in, each evaluator sets their own scores.
6. Only people set the scores. The total is worked out the same way every time, and AI never sets or changes a mark.
7. Compare bids brings each one to a single whole life figure. It does not rank them for you.
8. Record the award, then keep the supplier on track with scorecards and reviews.
9. Velstrian Buy. A clear process you can explain.

## Script 5: v-social-value (Leda)

1. Social value can carry real marks in a tender.
2. It is the wider good your work brings, such as good jobs, fair pay and skills.
3. Velstrian Bid is built around PPN 026, the government's social value guidance for central government work from January 2027.
4. Choose your measures and describe how you will deliver them. For private work, you answer the buyer's own questions the same way.
5. Velstrian Bid.
