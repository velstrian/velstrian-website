# Script: buyer long walkthrough

Title on screen: Run a fair, simple procurement with Velstrian Buy
Length target: 4 to 5 minutes
Voice: calm, plain, British English. No hype words. Jargon explained on first use.
Audience: a public or private buyer who runs their own procurements.

VO is the voice over. ON SCREEN is what the viewer sees. Blocks map to shot numbers in `shot-list.md`.

---

VO: Buying work fairly takes care. Velstrian Buy helps a buyer run a clear, even process, whether you are a public body or a private organisation. Here is the whole journey, from writing the requirement to managing the supplier after award.

ON SCREEN (shot 1): the buyer home screen.

---

VO: Start with the Requirement Builder. A requirement is what you are asking suppliers to deliver. The builder takes you through it step by step, and you can start from a template so you are not facing a blank page.

ON SCREEN (shot 2): the Requirement Builder stepper, starting from a template.

---

VO: Choose how the work goes to market and send it to suppliers. The requirement carries the procurement route and the send step.

ON SCREEN (shot 3): the requirement's procurement route and the send step. Note: this persona has no weighting editor on the requirement, so show the route and send, not a weighting editor that is not there. (Weighting and the rubric are covered as their own topic in the help centre and the b2 short where a persona has them.)

---

VO: Your pipeline shows your upcoming and current contracts in one place. For public buyers it also holds the views you must publish, including the pipeline notice under section 93 of the Procurement Act 2023, and a contract register you can export.

ON SCREEN (shot 4): the pipeline, the view switcher, the contract register export.

---

VO: When bids are in, evaluate. Each evaluator sets their own scores. The headline result is then worked out by a fixed calculation, the same every time, never by artificial intelligence. That keeps the outcome something you can stand behind.

ON SCREEN (shot 5): an evaluation, evaluator scores, the computed headline.

---

VO: To compare on price, use Compare bids. It brings each supplier's prices to a single whole life figure, the full cost over the life of the contract, one per currency. It does not rank the bids for you, that decision stays yours.

ON SCREEN (shot 6): the whole life figures side by side.

---

VO: Record the award. The evaluation shows the awarded outcome in one place.

ON SCREEN (shot 7): the Awarded state on the evaluation. Note: this persona shows Awarded with no standstill or letters step, so show the award state and do not imply a standstill flow that is not there. (The standstill and award letters are described in the help centre where a persona reaches them.)

---

VO: The work does not end at award. Open the supplier's record and keep it current.

ON SCREEN (shot 8): the supplier record after award.

---

VO: From there you can score a supplier with a scorecard, and hold regular reviews, both kept inside the supplier record.

ON SCREEN (shots 9 and 10): the scorecards and reviews areas. Note: on this persona both are empty states. Either cut these shots, or show the empty state and say in the voice over what a buyer sees before any scorecard or review exists. Do not create data to fill them.

---

VO: And track what the contract delivers with the Value tracker, built with finance in mind. It keeps its totals separate, so a delivered figure is never confused with a forecast. Your primary data is stored in the EU.

ON SCREEN (shot 11): the value tracker and its finance queue. If it is an empty state on this persona, describe what a buyer sees before any value is recorded.

---

VO: That is the full journey. Write the requirement, score it fairly, award it cleanly, and manage the supplier well. That is Velstrian Buy.

ON SCREEN: closing card, Velstrian Buy wordmark on the brand background.

---

Claims note for the reviewer: no win rate, accuracy figure, customer count or compliance promise. The deterministic headline line is important and is confirmed in `web/app/(dashboard)/buyer/evaluations/[id]/page.tsx:1-3`. Social value, where mentioned, is PPN 026 only. Data line is "primary data is stored in the EU". No payment step is shown.
