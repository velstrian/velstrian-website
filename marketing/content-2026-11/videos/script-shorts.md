# Script: short cuts

Each short is 30 to 90 seconds, one feature, silent first with burned in captions, so it works in a feed with the sound off. VO doubles as the caption text. Same routes and avoid rules as the shot list.

Format: ID, title, length, caption lines in order. A matching SRT is in `captions/`.

---

## s1-find Find the right tenders (35 s)
1. Looking for public tenders? Start here.
2. One search bar. Three official sources.
3. Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Public Contracts Scotland.
4. The closest matches rise to the top.
5. Save a search and new ones come to you.
6. Velstrian Bid.

## s2-upload Upload a tender pack (40 s)
1. Got the tender documents? Drop them in.
2. Velstrian Bid reads them into a clear list of questions.
3. Buyer changes during the tender stay in one place.
4. Always work from the latest version.
5. Velstrian Bid.

## s3-compliance Build the compliance checklist (45 s)
1. A compliance matrix is just a checklist of every question.
2. Break each question into its parts.
3. Give every part an owner.
4. Nothing slips through.
5. Velstrian Bid.

## s4-draft Draft an answer from your own evidence (60 s)
1. Your proof lives in the Evidence Vault.
2. Policies, certificates, case studies, in one place.
3. Velstrian Bid drafts a first answer from it.
4. A side drawer shows where every point came from.
5. You edit. You keep your voice.
6. Velstrian Bid.

## s5-social-value Add your social value answer (45 s)
1. Many tenders ask for social value.
2. The wider good your work brings. Local jobs. Lower carbon.
3. Velstrian Bid follows PPN 026, the current rule.
4. Choose your measures and describe them.
5. Velstrian Bid.

## s6-review-export Review and export the submission (45 s)
1. Check each answer against its evidence.
2. Bring in a colleague to review.
3. Sign it off.
4. Export a clean pack in Word and PDF.
5. Ready for the buyer's portal.
6. Velstrian Bid.

## s7-answer-library Reuse your best answers (40 s)
1. Wrote a strong answer once?
2. Keep it in the Answer library.
3. It is there for every future bid.
4. Pull it in, then tailor it to the buyer.
5. Velstrian Bid.

## b1-requirement Build a requirement (45 s)
1. Buying work? Start with the Requirement Builder.
2. A requirement is what you ask suppliers to deliver.
3. Step by step, or start from a template.
4. No blank page.
5. Velstrian Buy.

## b2-weighting Set the weighting and rubric (45 s)
1. Weighting is how much each part counts.
2. Price against quality, your call.
3. The rubric is the scale you score against.
4. Agree it up front. That is what keeps it fair.
5. Velstrian Buy.

## b3-evaluate Run an evaluation (60 s)
1. Bids are in. Time to score.
2. Each evaluator sets their own scores.
3. The headline is a fixed calculation.
4. The same every time. Never by AI.
5. An outcome you can stand behind.
6. Velstrian Buy.

## b4-compare Compare bids and whole life cost (50 s)
1. Comparing on price is hard when bids differ.
2. Compare bids brings each to one whole life figure.
3. The full cost over the life of the contract.
4. One per currency. It does not rank for you.
5. Velstrian Buy.

## b5-award Record the award and standstill (50 s)

4. And with the award letters.
5. Velstrian Buy.

## b6-scorecards Score a supplier after award (45 s)
