# Script: supplier long walkthrough

Title on screen: Win more work with Velstrian Bid
Length target: 4 to 5 minutes
Voice: calm, plain, British English. No hype words. Jargon explained on first use.
Audience: a small firm that writes its own bids, for public and private buyers.

Format of this script: VO is the voice over. ON SCREEN is what the viewer sees. Each block maps to a shot number in `shot-list.md`.

---

VO: Writing a bid by yourself is a lot of work. Velstrian Bid helps a small team do it well, without a bid department. Here is the whole journey, from finding a tender to sending your answer.

ON SCREEN (shot 1): the signed in home screen, the left menu.

---

VO: A tender is a formal invitation from a buyer asking suppliers to bid for a piece of work. Start on the Opportunities screen. One search bar covers three official sources: the Find a Tender Service, Contracts Finder and Public Contracts Scotland. Type what you do, and the closest matches come to the top, ranked against the sectors and regions on your profile.

ON SCREEN (shot 2): typing a search, the source chips, the ranked list.

---

VO: There is also an optional source for United States federal work, which uses your own key. Found a tender that fits and gives you enough time to write a proper answer? Start a bid straight from it, and the details carry across.

ON SCREEN (shot 3): the Start a bid step, details filled from the opportunity.

---

VO: Next, upload the tender pack. Buyers often issue changes during a live tender, called clarifications or addenda. Velstrian Bid keeps those in one place, in the Changes from the buyer section, so you always work from the latest version.

ON SCREEN (shot 4): uploading the pack, the Changes from the buyer section.

---

VO: The pack is read into a clear list of questions. Alongside it, the Requirements screen breaks the work into parts so you can see each one has an owner.

ON SCREEN (shots 5 and 6): the questions list, then the Requirements screen with owners. Note: on staging the compliance matrix route lands on Requirements, so this shot shows Requirements.

---

VO: Your proof lives in the Evidence Vault, your policies, certificates and case studies in one place. Link a piece of evidence to an answer, and it travels with the draft.

ON SCREEN (shot 7): linking a policy from the Evidence Vault.

---

VO: Now draft. On the Response page, Velstrian Bid writes a first answer grounded in your own evidence, and shows which document a point came from. You stay in control, you edit, and you keep your own voice.

ON SCREEN (shot 8): the Response page with a drafted answer and its source shown. Note: on staging the narrative route lands on the Response page, so this shot shows the Response page.

---

VO: Many public tenders ask for social value, the wider good your work brings, like local jobs or lower carbon. Velstrian Bid follows PPN 026, the current government rule, and helps you choose and describe your measures.

ON SCREEN (shot 9): the social value screen with PPN 026 measures.

---

VO: Before you send, review. Check each answer against the evidence, and bring in a colleague.

ON SCREEN (shot 10): the review screen. This persona has no sign off stages, so show the review step itself, do not imply a sign off that is not there.

---

VO: Finally, the submission. A pack you can export to Word and PDF, with your cover letter, ready for the buyer's portal. Your work is yours, and your primary data is stored in the EU.

ON SCREEN (shot 11): the submission cover letter section. Do NOT click export. STOP before any payment or portal checkout.

---

VO: That is the full journey. Find the right work, answer it with your own evidence, and send a clean, compliant submission. That is Velstrian Bid.

ON SCREEN: closing card, Velstrian Bid wordmark on the brand background.

---

Claims note for the reviewer: no win rate, accuracy figure, customer count or compliance promise is stated. Social value is PPN 026 only. The data line is "primary data is stored in the EU". The export shot stops before any payment step.
